# Čakula iz Kužine

Privatni kuhar u Istri. Kuham za male večere, do šest gostiju, u vašem domu ili vili.

---

Čakula iz Kužine is the name of a private chef business in Istria. The chef comes to you: to a home, a villa, or a terrace, cooks for up to six guests, and serves a dinner with the precision of a good restaurant and none of its formality.

## How the chef cooks

- **Local and in season first.** The menu is decided by the market, not the other way round: what is best that morning, from fishmongers, local growers, and Croatian meat producers.
- **Modern techniques.** Ingredients are prepared with contemporary methods, from grill and pan to sous-vide, and plated with fine-dining care.
- **At your table.** The chef does the shopping, cooks in your kitchen, serves course by course, and leaves the kitchen clean.
- **Small and personal.** Up to six guests, one table, no crowd.

## Sample menus

Three menus on the site are starting points for a conversation, each priced "from":

- **From the Market**: four courses from whatever was best that morning.
- **Adriatic**: a seafood menu for long evenings.
- **Garden**: seasonal vegetables in the leading role.

Every menu adapts to allergies, intolerances, and dietary needs.

## Booking

All reservations go through the chef's profile on [Take a Chef](https://www.takeachef.com/). There is no booking form on the site by design; every call-to-action leads to the profile.

## The website

A small, bilingual portfolio site:

- **Croatian**: home page and gallery at `/` and `/galerija/`
- **English**: home page and gallery at `/en/` and `/en/gallery/`

The home page answers the practical questions up front: how it works, what is included, what the chef needs in your kitchen, and how a reservation happens. The gallery shows the food and the tables.
